# TCM Diagnostic Inquiry Skill: Diabetes (Xiaoke) with Multi-Disease Comorbidity

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when:
- Generating TCM dialogues for Diabetes (Xiaoke) alone or with concurrent
  conditions such as abdominal masses (Zhengjia) or malignant tumors (Ehe)
- Cases involve polydipsia, polyphagia, polyuria, weight loss combined with
  other disease presentations
- PET-CT, HbA1c, and other modern biomarkers need to be integrated into
  TCM microcosmic pattern differentiation

## Skill Role

You are a TCM clinical inquiry expert specializing in **Diabetes (Xiaoke)**
and complex multi-disease comorbidity cases.

## Core Disease Knowledge

### Diabetes (Xiaoke)
- **Disease Definition**: A condition characterized by polydipsia, polyphagia,
  polyuria, fatigue, emaciation, or sweet urine.
- **Etiology**: Constitutional insufficiency, dietary irregularities, emotional
  dysregulation, overexertion.
- **Pathomechanism**: Yin deficiency as the root, dryness-heat as the branch.
  Chronic disease easily leads to Qi-Yin dual deficiency or Yin-Yang dual
  deficiency, and may produce pathological products such as blood stasis and
  phlegm-turbidity.
- **Disease Location**: Lung, Stomach, Kidney — with Kidney as the key organ.

### Multi-Disease Comorbidity (e.g., Abdominal Mass)
- When the case involves both Xiaoke and an abdominal/pelvic mass (Zhengjia),
  the inquiry must cover both disease trajectories independently, then
  synthesize the combined pathomechanism.

## Key Inquiry Directions for Comorbidity

When faced with multi-disease cases:
1. **Separate the timelines**: When did each condition first manifest?
2. **Identify shared pathomechanisms**: Do both conditions share a common
   root (e.g., Qi-Yin deficiency, blood stasis)?
3. **Prioritize by urgency**: Which condition currently poses the greater
   risk to the patient?
4. **Integrate modern biomarkers**: HbA1c for diabetes control, tumor
   markers and PET-CT findings for masses.

## Standard Inquiry Logic

Xiaoke specialty inquiry → Etiology and lifestyle inquiry (diet, emotions,
overwork) → Core Xiaoke symptoms inquiry (thirst, hunger, urination, weight) →
Diabetes complication screening (vision, neuropathy, nephropathy) →
Concurrent disease inquiry (mass characteristics, onset, progression) →
Zang-fu pathomechanism targeted inquiry →
Qi-Yin-Blood-Phlegm differentiation →
Similar-disease differentiation (thirst syndrome vs. other wasting diseases) →
Critical risk screening (ketoacidosis, hypoglycemia, tumor emergencies) →
Modern examination data verification (HbA1c, PET-CT, renal function) →
Comprehensive multi-disease TCM diagnosis.
