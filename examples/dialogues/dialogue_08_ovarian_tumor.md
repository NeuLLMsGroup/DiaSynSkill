# Dialogue Example 8: Ovarian Tumor (Zhengjia)

**Disease**: Ovarian Tumor (Zhengjia — Abdominal Mass)
**Department**: Gynecology / Oncology
**Syndrome Type**: Qi Stagnation with Blood Stasis and Phlegm-Dampness Accumulation
**Dialogue Rounds**: 13

---

**Patient**: Doctor, I was told I have an ovarian tumor. I'm 52 and haven't had any symptoms — it was found on a routine checkup. I want to understand this from a TCM perspective.

**Doctor**: When was it discovered? What imaging was done, and what did it show specifically?

**Patient**: Two months ago on ultrasound. Then I had an MRI. They said it's about 5 cm, partly cystic and partly solid. The CA-125 was slightly elevated.

**Doctor**: [Reviewing MRI report] I can see it's a complex adnexal mass. Now, even though you say you have no symptoms, let me ask some detailed questions. Many TCM signs are subtle and patients don't always connect them to the condition. How are your menstrual cycles — regular? Heavy or light flow? Painful?

**Patient**: I'm perimenopausal — my periods have been irregular for about a year. Sometimes heavy, sometimes I skip months. Some cramping but nothing severe.

**Doctor**: Do you experience lower abdominal distension, especially before your period? Any feeling of pressure or fullness in the pelvis?

**Patient**: Now that you mention it, I do feel a bit bloated — I thought it was just digestion.

**Doctor**: How are you emotionally? Do you tend to internalize stress? Any history of prolonged frustration or suppressed anger?

**Patient**: My husband and I divorced three years ago — it was very stressful. I do tend to hold things in.
