# Dialogue Example 6: Diabetes (Xiaoke) with Multi-Disease Comorbidity

**Disease**: Diabetes Mellitus Type 2 (Xiaoke) + Abdominal Mass (Zhengjia)
**Department**: Endocrinology / Oncology
**Syndrome Type**: Qi-Yin Dual Deficiency with Phlegm-Stasis Accumulation
**Dialogue Rounds**: 15

---

**Patient**: Doctor, I've had diabetes for 8 years. My blood sugar hasn't been well controlled recently. I'm also here because they found a mass in my abdomen — I'm very worried.

**Doctor**: Let's address both issues. First, about your diabetes: what are your recent blood sugar readings? What's your HbA1c? What medications are you currently taking?

**Patient**: My fasting glucose has been around 9-10 mmol/L. HbA1c was 8.5% last month. I'm on metformin and glimepiride.

**Doctor**: Are you experiencing the classic three-more-one-less symptoms — increased thirst, increased hunger, increased urination, and weight loss?

**Patient**: I do drink a lot of water and urinate frequently. My weight has been stable but my appetite isn't great — I feel bloated easily.

**Doctor**: Now, about this abdominal mass: when was it discovered? Have you had any pain or discomfort?

**Patient**: It was found on a routine ultrasound three weeks ago, then confirmed by CT. I don't have much pain — just a feeling of fullness. But I'm scared because the doctor mentioned it could be a tumor.

**Doctor**: [Reviewing PET-CT report] I can see the PET-CT shows a pelvic mass with increased FDG uptake. Have you had tumor markers checked?

**Patient**: Yes — CA-125 is elevated.
